01—04 What We Do
01

Trade Brokerage

INOW acts as an independent broker between buyers and sellers worldwide. We facilitate introductions, structure transactions, support negotiations, and help ensure smooth execution — creating win-win outcomes for all parties involved.

02

Industry Consultancy

Beyond trading, INOW provides strategic consultancy to companies operating in industrial and commercial sectors. We advise on market entry, sourcing strategies, operational optimization, and growth opportunities — backed by real-world trade experience.

03

Venture Capital & Angel Investments

INOW actively invests as a venture capital and angel investment firm. We support startups and early-stage companies with capital, strategic guidance, and access to our international business and trade ecosystem — focused on scalable ideas, strong founders, and long-term value creation.
